About Us

Our Story

Your Trusted Builder

A&DC Construction Corporation is a Filipino-owned and managed corporation established in 2022, specializing in Design and Engineering Construction. As a general contractor, we have the skills, resources and partnerships to provide value-added construction services while building lasting partnerships with our customers throughout the construction process.

Our logo is derived from the name of our owner, who worked as a construction worker for many years and dreamed of having his own company. The gold and the building symbolize our goal: to build your dreams, exceeding your expectations with exceptional quality and pride.

Our Commitments

Vision, Mission & Values

To provide a reliable and efficient service to our clients on every project.

To be a leading construction company in the global market.

To become the customer’s most choice by attaining excellence in quality and timely completed value-added projects.

Dedicated to providing quality construction, technical and management services to our valued customers.

We strive to build long-term relationships with our clients based on safety, quality, timely service and an anticipation of their needs.

We will treat all employees fairly and involve them in the quality improvement process to ensure responsiveness and cost-effective work execution.

We value excellence, trust, respect, empowerment, accountability and mutual relationship.

We maintain the highest level of professionalism, integrity, honesty and fairness in our relationships with suppliers, subcontractors and clients.

Health & Safety

A safe workplace, every day

Company’s responsibility. A&DC shall provide and maintain a working environment that is safe and without risk to the health and safety of its employees and assets.

Employee’s responsibility. Every employee shall comply with all relevant legislation, instructions, rules and procedures, and share responsibility for everyone’s safety while using equipment properly.

We are committed to:

  • Protect our own health and safety
  • Protect the health and safety of others
  • Care for and correctly use personal protective equipment
  • Promptly report any risk to any person
  • Co-operate to ensure compliance with duties
  • Comply with prescribed safety measures
  • Maintain mental health and a positive attitude
