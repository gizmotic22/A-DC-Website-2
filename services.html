How We Work

From First Discussion to Turnover

Every project follows a structured process, from consultation and planning to construction and handover.

01

Consultation & Site Assessment

We listen to the client's goals, assess site conditions, and establish the initial project scope, budget range, and timeline.

02

Design & Engineering

Project plans, drawings, and technical specifications are developed based on the agreed scope and requirements.

03

Construction & Supervision

Construction proceeds according to the agreed scope, with coordination, progress monitoring, and attention to workmanship.

04

Testing & Turnover

The completed work is inspected and applicable systems are tested. Outstanding items are addressed before handover to the client.
